Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026. This policy explains how Raymond Digital collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit this website or contact us about our services.

1. Overview

Raymond Digital (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Raymond Digital website (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy informs you regarding our privacy practices and the information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

By accessing or using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal details: name, email address, and phone number when you submit a contact form or enquiry.
  • Service preferences: the type of service you are interested in and your project description. You may also provide an optional estimated budget range, which is submitted and stored with your enquiry in our Supabase contact database for enquiry handling and administration.
  • Communication records: correspondence between you and Raymond Digital, including email and WhatsApp messages.
  • Technical data: our website does not use its own tracking code to collect browser, device, or IP information. Technical information may still be processed by third-party infrastructure and service providers as part of operating the website and delivering form submissions.

We do not collect payment information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested services;
  • Communicate about ongoing projects, quotations, and service delivery;
  • Maintain basic business records for operational purposes.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information where necessary:

  • To deliver a service you have requested (e.g., third-party email delivery services for contact form submissions);
  • Where required by law, regulation, or legal process;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Raymond Digital, our clients, or others.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

We maintain reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request that we update or correct your details;
  • Request that we delete your personal information;
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at raymonddigitalx@gmail.com or via the details on our contact page.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Raymond Digital public website does not intentionally use cookies or analytics and tracking technologies for visitor tracking. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. If our use of cookies or tracking technologies changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

8. Third-Party Services

Contact information you submit may be processed through Formspree for form processing and email delivery. Enquiry information is also stored using Supabase database services so that we can manage and respond to enquiries; access to stored enquiries is restricted using authentication and database access controls for our private administration area.

Website fonts and libraries are delivered through third-party content networks. These deliver static assets and do not process your enquiry information. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services. You may review their policies directly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. You are encouraged to review this page periodically for any changes. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
